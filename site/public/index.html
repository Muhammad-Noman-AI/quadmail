Your own email server.
On your Cloudflare. For free.

QuadMail gives your domain a complete mail system: receiving, sending, webmail and a team. It runs on your own Cloudflare account, so your mail stays yours. Deploy with one click, then a wizard sets up the rest.

View the code

About 20 minutes. No server, no command line.

Receive everything

Cloudflare Email Routing hands every mail for your domain to QuadMail. Catch-all included, so any address works.

Send that lands in the inbox

Sending through Resend with SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up for you. Delivery, bounces and complaints are tracked.

A real webmail

Threads, search, labels, drafts, attachments, replies and forwards. Dark mode, keyboard shortcuts, installable on your phone.

Your whole team

Add people with their own mailbox and password. Shared addresses like sales@. Each person sees only their own mail.

Safe by default

Password plus optional two-factor login. Email HTML is sanitised and shown without scripts; remote images are blocked until you allow them.

Yours, really

Mail, keys and passwords live in your Cloudflare account. Nobody else, including us, can read them. Updates are one button.

How it works

  1. 1

    Deploy

    Click Deploy to Cloudflare. Cloudflare copies QuadMail to your GitHub, creates the database and storage, and starts it. You choose a setup code.

  2. 2

    Set up

    Open your new QuadMail. The wizard asks for the setup code, creates your admin account, connects Resend, shows the DNS records to add, and checks them.

  3. 3

    Go

    Point Email Routing's catch-all at QuadMail, send a test, add your team. New versions arrive through a weekly update in your GitHub copy.

What you need

  • A domain whose DNS is on Cloudflare
  • A Cloudflare account (the free plan; Cloudflare may ask for a card to turn on R2 storage)
  • A free Resend account for sending
  • A free GitHub account

Free-plan limits

QuadMail is built to stay inside these free limits, and its Usage page warns you at 80%.

Sending (Resend Free)100 mails per day, 3,000 per month, 3 domains
Receiving (Email Routing)Unlimited; up to 25 MiB per mail
Mail storage (R2)10 GB
Database (D1)500 MB, roughly 30,000 to 50,000 messages
App (Workers)100,000 requests per day

Need more? Resend Pro raises sending to 50,000 per month; Cloudflare's paid plans raise the rest. Nothing in QuadMail changes.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, within the limits above. QuadMail itself is free software (AGPL-3.0). You use your own free Cloudflare and Resend accounts; we never bill you.

Can Quadgentics read my mail?

No. QuadMail runs entirely in your Cloudflare account. We have no access to it, and the setup never sends us your keys or passwords.

Can I use Outlook or the Gmail app?

Not yet: QuadMail is webmail and has no IMAP/SMTP. You can install it as an app on your phone or computer from the browser.

What happens to my existing mail?

Mail stays wherever it is now. QuadMail receives new mail from the moment you point Email Routing at it. Importing old mail is not part of version 1.

How do updates work?

Your GitHub copy has an Update QuadMail workflow. It runs every Monday (or when you press Run) and installs the latest release; Cloudflare then deploys it.

Something went wrong. Where do I get help?

Open an issue on GitHub. The README and setup guide cover the manual way too.
